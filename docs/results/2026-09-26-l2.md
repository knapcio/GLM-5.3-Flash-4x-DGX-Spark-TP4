# LVKP-S-L2: 26 September 2026

Fresh **sparkDash DecodeBench** on the restored current recipe measured **70.19
tokens/s prose c1** (median of 5) and **152.89 tokens/s aggregate prose c4**
(median of 3). These are decode rates, excluding the initial token from the
decode-token count. The recipe retains NVFP4 routed experts and the existing
`lossless8` profile for 8-bit non-expert weights; that profile name does not
describe a mathematically lossless conversion.

All 20 planned jobs completed: 2 discarded warmups and 18 scored runs. Independent
raw review verified 20 unique jobs, the exact requested matrix, and all 100
streams with 256 completion tokens, 255 decode tokens, zero reasoning chunks,
and no errors. Every published median was independently recomputed from the
retained per-job values. The collector source hash matched the executed source.

“Per-stream” is the median of the job's `meanDecodeTps` field; aggregate is the
median of `aggregateDecodeTps`. They are different dashboard measurements, so
aggregate need not equal concurrency multiplied by mean stream rate. Rows with
one run are supplementary observations, not repeated headline estimates.

An earlier sparkDash session on the same accepted recipe recorded prose c1
70.84 (median of 5) and prose c4 aggregate 151.41 (median of 3). The fresh session
differs by -0.65 and +1.48 tokens/s, respectively. These are separate sessions;
the difference establishes neither a causal speedup nor a regression.

recipe and **were not rerun with this fresh performance matrix**:

- **qeval:** 75/75 at c1 and 75/75 at c4, zero truncated tasks. Retained raw
  outputs were independently checked again for the complete task grid and scores.
- **KLD probe:** mean **0.0288366157**, 17 calibration items and 6,618
  teacher-forced positions. The retained raw distributions and exact lengths
  were structurally revalidated and the value recomputed. This is the existing
  top-20 folded-tail estimate, not full-vocabulary KL; the original execution
  receipt was not reconstructed after the fact.
- **Long-context registry:** retained `PASS_SANITY` 32/32 at c1 and 32/32 at c4,
  with 9,597–9,642 prompt tokens. This entry comes from the saved qualification
  verification log; it is a sanity check, not a complete long-context accuracy
  benchmark or a fresh rerun.
